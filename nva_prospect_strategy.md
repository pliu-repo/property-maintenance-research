# Northern Virginia Multifamily Prospects & Outreach Strategy

## Your Interview Goals
- 20-30 initial outreach attempts
- Book 10-15 conversations in next 2-3 weeks
- Mix: small multifamily owners + property managers + maintenance coordinators

## Tier 1: Local Organizations (Highest Leverage)
These groups have built-in networks of your exact target audience.

### 1. Northern Virginia Multifamily Association
- Local chapter of NARPM (National Association of Residential Property Managers)
- Contact: Search "NARPM Northern Virginia" or Virginia Apartment & Multifamily Housing Association
- Action: Join the association, attend meetings, ask for member directory or intro calls
- Why: Direct access to property managers managing 10-100 unit properties

### 2. Real Estate Investor Associations
- Northern Virginia Real Estate Investors Association (NVREIA)
- Arlington/Alexandria Real Estate Investment Groups
- Fairfax County Landlord Association
- Action: Attend a meeting, ask for intro to small multifamily owners
- Why: Mix of owners and operators

### 3. Small Business / Chamber Groups
- Fairfax Chamber of Commerce
- Arlington Chamber of Commerce
- McLean Chamber
- Action: Network events, member lists
- Why: Local business owners managing properties

## Tier 2: LinkedIn Outreach (Most Scalable)
Search these exact terms in Northern Virginia:

### Search 1: "Property Manager" + Northern Virginia
- Filter: "Property Manager" title, companies managing 10-100 unit buildings
- Example companies to search:
  - Local independent property management firms
  - Regional players with smaller portfolios
  - Single-property owner-operators

### Search 2: "Multifamily Property Manager"
- Focus on those managing smaller portfolios
- Look for recent movers (changed jobs recently = more open)

### Search 3: "Apartment Manager" + "Alexandria" OR "Arlington" OR "Fairfax"
- On-site managers often do maintenance coordination
- Good secondary interviews

### Search 4: "Owner Operator" + "Residential" + Northern Virginia
- Direct to owners

### Search 5: "Maintenance Supervisor" + "Apartments"
- People doing the actual work

## Tier 3: Local Property Management Companies (Direct Research)
Look these up directly by city:

### Alexandria
- Local property management firms (Google: "property management companies Alexandria VA 10-100 units")
- Owner-operator groups
- Small property management offices

### Arlington
- Similar search, smaller firms preferred

### Fairfax
- Larger concentration of small-medium operators
- More fragmented market = more prospect companies

### Leesburg / Falls Church / Reston / Herndon
- Growing markets with smaller independent operators
- Often less sophisticated on maintenance workflows

**Action:** Build a list of 20-30 local companies, get founder/owner names, reach out directly.

## Tier 4: Vendors & Referral Sources (Warm Intros)
These people work with your target customers daily.

### Plumbers / Electricians
- Local plumbing companies
- HVAC contractors
- Handyman services
- "Who are your top 5 property management clients?"

### Contractors
- General contractors specializing in residential
- Unit turnover companies

### Real Estate Brokers
- Local multifamily brokers
- Residential brokerage firms
- "Who are your small multifamily owner clients?"

### Property Accountants / Bookkeepers
- CPAs specializing in real estate
- Bookkeeping services for small landlords
- "Who do you serve managing 10-100 units?"

**Action:** Join groups, introduce yourself, ask for 10 minutes with an owner or manager.

## Your Outreach Sequence

### Week 1: Prep
- Join 2-3 online communities
- Search LinkedIn and bookmark 30-50 prospects
- Research 10-15 local PM companies and get founder names
- Ask your personal network for 2-3 warm intros

### Week 2: Reach Out
- Send 10-15 LinkedIn messages
- Send 10-15 cold emails to PM company founders
- Contact 3-5 vendors/brokers asking for intro
- Follow up on personal network intros
- Post in 2-3 Facebook groups asking for conversation

### Week 3: Follow Up & Book
- Follow up on Week 2 messages (50% won't respond first time)
- Attend 1 property manager or investor meetup if possible
- Book calls as confirmations come in

## Success Metrics for Week 1-3
- 30+ outreach attempts
- 10-15 conversation confirmations
- 8-12 actual calls booked
- Clear pain patterns emerging from interviews
- 1-2 warm referrals for additional conversations

## Key Insight
Northern Virginia is fragmented enough that small multifamily owners and property managers are:
- NOT using enterprise systems like AppFolio
- Managing maintenance manually
- Willing to talk about workflow pain
- Good early customers

Your job: find them and ask good questions.
