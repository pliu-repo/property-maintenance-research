# Week 1 Action Plan: Getting Started

## Your Goal This Week
Build a prospect list and start outreach, with aim to book 3-5 first calls by end of week.

## Daily Breakdown

### Monday & Tuesday (Today + Tomorrow)
**Task: Research & List Building (4-5 hours total)**

1. LinkedIn searches (2 hours)
   - Search "Property Manager" + Northern Virginia
   - Search "Multifamily Property Manager"  
   - Search "Owner Operator" + Residential
   - Bookmark 30-50 profiles
   - Focus on people at small companies (5-50 employees)

2. Google research (1.5 hours)
   - Search "property management companies Fairfax VA"
   - Search "property management companies Alexandria VA"
   - Search "apartment management companies Arlington VA"
   - Build list of 20-30 companies
   - Get founder/owner names and emails
   - Use LinkedIn to find email addresses

3. Personal network reach out (30 min)
   - Email 2-5 people you know
   - Ask for 1-2 introductions to property managers or small multifamily owners
   - Subject: "Do you know any small multifamily property managers in NoVA?"

### Wednesday & Thursday
**Task: Community Signup & Initial Outreach (3-4 hours)**

1. Join online communities (30 min)
   - Join 2-3 Facebook groups (Northern Virginia Landlords, Fairfax Landlord Association, etc.)
   - Introduce yourself

2. First wave of outreach (2.5-3.5 hours)
   - Send 10-15 LinkedIn messages (high response rate, easiest)
   - Start with property managers and owners
   - Spread them out: don't send all at once
   - Use Template 1 (Property Manager) mostly
   - Personalize 2-3 with specific details

3. Post in 1-2 Facebook groups (15 min)
   - Use the Facebook template provided
   - Mention you want to learn about their maintenance workflow

### Friday
**Task: Consolidate & Prepare for Week 2 (2-3 hours)**

1. Send first cold emails (1.5-2 hours)
   - Target 10-15 PM company owners/founders
   - Use the cold email templates
   - Personalize subject line with city or company size

2. Track responses and follow-ups (30 min)
   - Record all outreach in prospect tracker
   - Flag responses
   - Plan follow-ups for Week 2

3. Send warm intro requests (30 min)
   - Reach out to 2-3 vendors/brokers
   - Use the warm intro template
   - Ask them to introduce you to 1-2 customers

## By End of Week 1
✅ 30-50 total outreach attempts
✅ 3-5 confirmed calls booked for Week 2
✅ Responses coming in for follow-ups
✅ 1-2 warm intro requests in flight

## Week 2 Preview
- Follow up on Week 1 messages (50% won't respond first time)
- Send second wave of cold outreach (10-15 more emails)
- Attend 1 property manager meetup if possible
- Conduct first 3-5 customer discovery calls
- Take detailed notes on workflows and pain points

## Success Indicators
✅ Getting 15-25% response rate on LinkedIn
✅ Getting 1-2 responses per 10 emails sent
✅ Getting 1-2 DMs from Facebook posts
✅ Getting 1-2 warm intro offers from vendors

If response rates are lower:
- Try different message variations
- Personalize more
- Follow up earlier
- Try different times of day

## Tools You'll Need
- LinkedIn account
- Email account
- Prospect tracker spreadsheet (provided)
- Zoom or phone for calls
- Calendar (Calendly or Google Calendar link to share)

## Key Mindset
- This is not selling, it's learning
- You're doing them a favor asking their opinion
- 80% rejection is normal
- Every "no" gets you closer to "yes"
- Quality of learning matters more than quantity of calls
