# 1. WEEK 1 ACTION PLAN

## Goal
Build a prospect list and book 3-5 first calls by end of week.

### Monday & Tuesday: Research & List Building (4-5 hours)
**LinkedIn Searches (2 hours)**
- Search: "Property Manager" + Northern Virginia
- Search: "Multifamily Property Manager"
- Search: "Owner Operator" + Residential + Virginia
- Search: "Apartment Manager" + "Alexandria" OR "Arlington" OR "Fairfax"
- Search: "Maintenance Supervisor" + Apartments + Virginia
- Bookmark 30-50 profiles
- Focus on people at companies with 5-50 employees
- Note: Look for people who changed jobs recently (more open to conversation)

**Google Research (1.5 hours)**
- Search: "property management companies Fairfax VA"
- Search: "property management companies Alexandria VA"
- Search: "apartment management companies Arlington VA"
- Search: "property management companies Reston VA"
- Build list of 20-30 local companies
- Get founder/owner names from websites and LinkedIn
- Find email addresses using LinkedIn or company websites

**Personal Network Outreach (30 min)**
- Email 2-5 people you know
- Subject: "Do you know any small multifamily property managers in NoVA?"
- Ask for 1-2 introductions to property managers or small multifamily owners
- Keep it casual and brief

### Wednesday & Thursday: Community & First Outreach (3-4 hours)
**Join Online Communities (30 min)**
- Join Facebook: "Northern Virginia Landlords"
- Join Facebook: "Fairfax County Landlord Association"
- Join Facebook: "Arlington Real Estate Investors"
- Join Facebook: "Alexandria Rental Property Owners"
- Join Facebook: "Northern Virginia Real Estate Investing"
- Write short introduction in each group

**First Wave of LinkedIn Messages (2-2.5 hours)**
- Send 10-15 LinkedIn messages
- Spread throughout the day (don't send all at once)
- Mix: 70% Property Manager template, 20% Owner/Operator, 10% personalized
- Personalize at least 2-3 with specific details (company name, city, property count)
- Best times: Tue-Thu, 9-11am or 2-4pm EST

**Facebook Group Posts (15 min)**
- Post in 2-3 groups using provided Facebook templates
- Don't hard-sell, just ask for conversation partners
- Check back later to respond to DMs

### Friday: Consolidate & Prepare for Week 2 (2-3 hours)
**Send First Wave of Cold Emails (1.5-2 hours)**
- Target 10-15 PM company owners/founders
- Use cold email templates provided
- Personalize subject lines with city or company size
- Best times: Tue-Wed, 10am-12pm EST

**Track & Organize (30 min)**
- Enter all outreach attempts into prospect tracker
- Flag any responses
- Plan follow-ups for Week 2

**Send Warm Intro Requests (30 min)**
- Reach out to 2-3 vendors/brokers using warm intro template
- Examples: plumbers, electricians, contractors, real estate brokers
- Ask them to introduce you to 1-2 small multifamily customers

## By End of Week 1 - Target Outcomes
✅ 30-50 total outreach attempts
✅ 3-5 confirmed calls booked for Week 2
✅ Responses coming in for follow-ups
✅ 1-2 warm intro requests in flight
✅ First patterns emerging about pain points

## Week 2 Preview
- Follow up on Week 1 messages (send 2nd message to non-responders)
- Send second wave of outreach (10-15 more emails)
- Attend 1 property manager meetup if available
- Conduct first 3-5 customer discovery calls
- Take detailed notes on workflows and pain points

# 2. NORTHERN VIRGINIA PROSPECT RESEARCH STRATEGY

## Tier 1: Local Organizations (Highest Leverage)
These have built-in networks of your exact target customers.

### Northern Virginia Multifamily Association
- Local chapter of NARPM (National Association of Residential Property Managers)
- Search: "NARPM Northern Virginia" or "Virginia Apartment & Multifamily Housing Association"
- Action: Join, attend meetings, request member directory
- Value: Direct access to 50-100+ property managers

### Real Estate Investor Associations
- Northern Virginia Real Estate Investors Association (NVREIA)
- Arlington/Alexandria Real Estate Investment Groups
- Fairfax County Landlord Association
- Action: Attend meetings, network, ask for intros
- Value: Mix of owners and local operators

### Chamber of Commerce & Small Business Groups
- Fairfax Chamber of Commerce
- Arlington Chamber of Commerce
- McLean Chamber
- Action: Network events, member directories
- Value: Local business owners managing properties

## Tier 2: LinkedIn (Most Scalable)

### Search Terms to Use
1. "Property Manager" + Northern Virginia
   - Filter: Company size 5-50 employees
   - Filter: Recent position changes (1-2 years)

2. "Multifamily Property Manager" 
   - Sort by: Recently active
   - Look for: Independent or small firm backgrounds

3. "Apartment Manager" + Alexandria/Arlington/Fairfax
   - Focus on: On-site managers (do maintenance coordination)
   - Good for: Secondary/validation interviews

4. "Owner Operator" + "Residential" + Northern Virginia
   - Direct to owners
   - Usually more willing to talk

5. "Maintenance Supervisor" + "Apartments"
   - People doing the actual work
   - Great for understanding operational pain

### LinkedIn Outreach Strategy
- Send 5-10 messages per day (not all at once)
- Personalize at least 30% of messages
- Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- Best times: 9-11am or 2-4pm EST
- Follow up after 5 days if no response
- Expected response rate: 15-25%

## Tier 3: Direct Company Research
Look up companies directly by city.

### Alexandria
- Search Google: "property management companies Alexandria VA"
- Look for: Companies managing 10-100 unit buildings
- Get: Founder/owner names, email, phone

### Arlington  
- Search Google: "apartment management companies Arlington VA"
- Same targeting: small-medium independent firms

### Fairfax
- Largest concentration of small-medium operators
- More fragmented = more opportunities
- Search: "property management Fairfax VA"

### Leesburg / Falls Church / Reston / Herndon
- Growing markets with smaller independent operators
- Often less sophisticated on maintenance workflows

**Action:** Build list of 20-30 companies, get founder names, reach out directly via email.

## Tier 4: Vendors & Referral Sources (Warm Intros)
People who work with your target customers daily.

### Types to Contact
- Plumbers, electricians, HVAC contractors
- General contractors, handyman services, unit turnover companies
- Real estate brokers (residential and multifamily)
- CPAs and bookkeepers specializing in real estate
- Property maintenance companies, cleaning services

### What to Ask Them
- "Who are 3-5 small multifamily property managers you work with?"
- "What operational challenges do they mention most often?"
- "Would you introduce me to one or two for a quick conversation?"

### Value
- 40-50% response rate on warm intro requests
- Usually respond within 24-48 hours
- Can lead to 2-3 high-quality conversations

## Tier 5: Facebook & Online Communities

### Groups to Join
- Northern Virginia Landlords
- Fairfax County Landlord Association
- Arlington Real Estate Investors
- Alexandria Rental Property Owners
- Northern Virginia Real Estate Investing
- Local city-specific landlord/investor groups

### Strategy
- Join groups
- Introduce yourself naturally
- Post research question in 2-3 groups
- Direct message anyone who responds
- Expected: 2-5 direct messages per post

## Key Messages to Copy-Paste


### Facebook - Quick Version
```
Hi everyone, researching how small multifamily owners manage maintenance. Would 
anyone be open to a quick 15-min call about your process? Interested in work 
orders, vendor coordination, cost tracking.
```

## Expected Response Rates
- LinkedIn messages: 15-25% response rate
- Cold emails: 10-15% response rate  
- Warm intro requests: 40-50% response rate
- Facebook posts: 2-5 direct messages per post

## Best Outreach Times
- LinkedIn: Tue-Thu, 9-11am or 2-4pm EST
- Email: Tue-Wed, 10am-12pm EST
- Facebook: Evening or weekends (post at 6-8pm)

## Tone Checklist
✅ DO:
- Be specific about what you want to learn
- Keep it short (5-7 sentences max)
- Ask for exactly 15 minutes
- Be respectful of their time
- Use their language (work orders, vendors, maintenance, approvals)
- Sound like a researcher, not a salesperson

❌ DON'T:
- Pitch your idea or product
- Use corporate-speak ("synergy," "disrupt," "innovative")
- Ask vague questions
- Say "I have a startup idea"
- Use "AI" or "technology" language
- Make it about you — make it about learning from them

# 10. SUCCESS METRICS & TRACKING

## Week 1 Goals
- 30-50 total outreach attempts
- 15-25% response rate on LinkedIn (4-6 messages)
- 10-15% response rate on emails (1-2 responses)
- 2-5 Facebook DMs
- 3-5 calls booked for Week 2

## Week 2-3 Goals
- Complete 8-12 customer discovery calls
- Clear pattern emerging on top 2-3 pain points
- 1-2 warm referrals to additional conversations
- Refined understanding of workflow

## Call Quality Metrics
- Average call length: 20-30 minutes (some will run long, that's good)
- Caller willingness to share details: High (if they're engaged)
- Pain point clarity: High (you understand the problem)
- Follow-up interest: Will they introduce you to others?

## Red Flags to Adjust
- If response rate < 10% on LinkedIn: Try more personalization
- If response rate < 5% on emails: Try different subject lines
- If no Facebook engagement: Try different groups or times
- If getting low-quality calls: Refine your targeting

---

## KEY MINDSET FOR SUCCESS

1. **This is research, not selling**
   - You're learning, not pitching
   - They're doing YOU a favor

2. **Rejection is normal**
   - 80% rejection rate is standard
   - Every "no" gets you closer to "yes"

3. **Quality over quantity**
   - 5 great conversations > 20 superficial ones
   - Listen more than you talk

4. **You're looking for patterns**
   - Don't over-react to one person's opinion
   - Look for consensus across 8-12 conversations

5. **This work compounds**
   - Each conversation gets better
   - Referrals are gold
   - One good interview can lead to 3-5 more

---

1. **Conduct calls** with booked prospects
