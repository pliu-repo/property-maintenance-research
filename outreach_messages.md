---

## TONE GUIDANCE

✅ DO:
- Be specific about what you want to learn
- Keep it short (5-7 sentences max)
- Ask for exactly 15 minutes
- Be respectful of their time
- Use their language (work orders, vendors, maintenance, approvals)
- Sound like a researcher, not a salesperson

❌ DON'T:
- Pitch your idea or product
- Use corporate-speak ("synergy," "disrupt," "innovative")
- Ask vague questions like "what do you think of property management?"
- Say "I have a startup idea"
- Use "AI" or "technology" language
- Make it about you — make it about learning from them

---

## EXPECTED RESPONSE RATES & TIMING

**LinkedIn messages:** 15-25% response rate
- Best day: Tue-Thu
- Best time: 9-11am or 2-4pm
- Follow up after 5 days if no response

**Cold emails:** 10-15% response rate
- Best day: Tue-Wed
- Best time: 10am-12pm
- Follow up after 4-5 days

**Warm intros/referrals:** 40-50% response rate
- Usually within 24-48 hours

**Facebook posts:** 2-5 direct messages per post
- Post in evening or weekend
- Bump it with a comment after a few hours

---
